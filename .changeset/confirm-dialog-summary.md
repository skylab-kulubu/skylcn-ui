ConfirmDialog lists what happens (`consequences`) beside what stays (`unaffected`), can ask for a typed phrase (`confirmText`) that ticks once it matches and confirms on Enter, and can hold its action for `delay` seconds behind a draining line. It takes an `icon` in a new header band with a close button, opens wide when it carries a summary or a phrase, and rises as a sheet on phones.
