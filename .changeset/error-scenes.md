New ErrorScene draws the club's moving picture for a whole-page error, the same for each kind in every product: each a moment a member knows from the club: 404 GECEKODU at 03:47 with "404" in the terminal and the mugs empty, 500 the ARTLAB robot (after Kaan's 2026 ARTLAB figure) watching a training run blow up at step 23, 403 a guessed SkyDays CTF flag refused, offline YıldızJam's jam paused with the plug out, an expired session GECEKODU's pranks on a laptop left unlocked (file bomb, fake format) and maintenance WEBLAB deploying. The words name the team in the sentence, in Turkish and English. One line language in theme colours for light and dark, inline SVG with CSS animation, no inline style and nothing random, still under reduced motion or `animated={false}`, hidden from screen readers. Each scene is its own export (NotFoundScene, ServerErrorScene, ForbiddenScene, OfflineScene, SessionExpiredScene, MaintenanceScene) so a page ships only its own; `errorSceneText(kind, locale)` gives the default title, line, code and button. StatusPage takes `scene`, drawn in place of the large code, which turns into a small line under it; TaskPage takes `top`, a picture above the title.
