Avatar stays round by default, and the README says why: the shape tells a person from the rounded-square interface around it and from teams, groups and events in the same lists, faces and initials sit better in a circle, and it is what GitHub, Fluent, Atlassian and Linear do. `shape="square"` stays for a tile that stands for a team or a service. Where a round avatar sits among rounded squares it matches them in box size, border and ring tokens and vertical centre instead of changing shape. The sidebar's account row and AvatarGroup's "+N" are round too.
