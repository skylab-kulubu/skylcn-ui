New RelativeTime says a moment from now, such as "3 saat önce" or "yarın", in the provider's language, kept current by one shared 30-second clock, with the full date and time in a Tooltip on hover (`tooltip` picks the side, `false` uses the title) and the exact moment in its `datetime`. `formatRelativeTime` gives the same words as a string.
