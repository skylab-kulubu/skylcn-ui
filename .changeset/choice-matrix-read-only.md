ChoiceMatrix takes `readOnly` to show answers already given, such as a submitted response, with no controls: the table with filled marks where it fits ("Seçili" for screen readers), else each row beside its answer, with "Cevap yok" for a row left empty. `multiple` draws square marks and joins a row's answers.
