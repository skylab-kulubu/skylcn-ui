Area and line charts always show their first and last dates, pulled in from the plot's edges when they would spill, where the first one used to drop out. Their points are smaller and drop the outline in the card's colour, which showed as a halo once a chart sat outside its card. The first chart colour, which a single series takes, leans halfway to the brand's own lilac (`--chart-1` is `#dcbef2` in dark and `#8c5fc7` in light), so it reads on brand; BarList's first bar, the Sparkline dot and the first avatar tint follow it.
