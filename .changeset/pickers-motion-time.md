The calendar moves like forms' calendar: months slide, the month and year views slide in vertically with a blur, and picker panels rise into place. Its header is a plain row, it always shows six weeks, the chosen day is tinted and today has a dot. New TimePicker picks hours and minutes from two columns; DateTimePicker uses the same columns; DatePicker gains Bugün and Temizle; and the picker triggers take `size` and a 26px `variant="pill"`.
