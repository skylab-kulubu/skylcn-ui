Box controls come in three sizes: sm 32px, md 36px (the default) and lg 38px, taller on touch screens, set per control or once for a whole form with the new ControlSizeProvider. Input (`inputSize`), Textarea and SearchInput take them, with a see-through fill, rounder corners above sm and a lilac focus ring. Input grows from 32px to 36px by default.
