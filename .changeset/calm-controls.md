Controls weigh less. Button's outline variant sits on a wash instead of nothing, and a button's icons follow its size (14px on sm and md, 16px on lg) instead of 16px everywhere; IconButton gains an icon-xs size, 24px. SegmentedControl, FilterPills and Pagination mark the chosen item with a neutral raised chip instead of a lilac one, and their labels, Select's value and Badge's text drop to regular weight, the way an Input's text reads. Pagination's current page stays square however many digits it has.
