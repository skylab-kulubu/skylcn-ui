DataList draws itself from `rows` and column definitions: give each column its `header` (or a spoken `label`), `cell`, `sortable`, `interactive` and `className`, and the header, rows, skeleton (`loading`) and empty state (`empty`) follow, in the columns' order; adding or dropping a column is one entry in the array. A column with a `sortValue` sorts the rows in place, text A to Z first, figures and dates largest first, empty values last either way, starting from `defaultSort` unless the page keeps `sort` itself; rows that arrive sorted, such as one page from a server, leave `sortValue` out. Rows link through `rowHref`, act through `onRowSelect` or open a panel across the list's width through `renderPanel`, with a chevron column at the start, `canExpand` per row and the open row's id kept by the list (`expandedId`, `onExpandedChange`) or by the page, such as in the address. `fill` lets the list take the height left in a flex column and scroll its rows under the header from md up. A column's `width` can change by breakpoint (`{ base: 'auto', lg: '5rem' }`). Column headers drop the spaced capitals for plain small text. A list now comes in as one quick, even wave when it first fills, top to bottom within 150ms however many rows there are, instead of 60ms a row with every row past the eighth landing at once; DataListBody numbers its rows itself, so `DataListRow`'s `index` does nothing and is deprecated. A list drawn from `rows` redraws a row only when its row, the columns or its open state change, so typing, opening a row or a parent's render leaves the other rows alone (keep `columns` stable and rebuild it when what the cells read changes); rows measure their places for the sort glide only when their order changes, and sorting reuses one collator. `useDataListPanelOpened` tells a panel's content when the panel has finished opening, so heavy content such as a chart can wait for it and the motion stays smooth; the headers are not drawn again when a row opens. Lists put together from parts keep working, and their `DataListHeader`, `DataListColumnHeader` and `DataListCell` now render nothing for a column the list does not have, so columns can come and go at run time; the row link moves past interactive columns instead of being clipped inside one. `DataListRow` takes `expanded`, `onExpandedChange` and `panel`, and now wraps its grid row and panel in one element, which takes the row's `className`, `style` and other props. New parts: `DataListActions` for quick actions that open out on hover or focus where there is a mouse and always show on touch screens (`hoverFrom` to start at a breakpoint), `DataListTitle` for a title with a line under it and an icon or avatar before it, `DataListExpander` for the chevron, and `DataListFooter` for the count and paging under a list, which stays at the bottom of the screen while a long list scrolls.
