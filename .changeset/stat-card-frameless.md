`StatCard` takes `framed={false}` to set a figure straight into a Section or a row of figures, without the card's border, fill and padding; its lines then sit closer, so each figure still reads as one. Its label is now plain text instead of letter-spaced capitals, framed or not, and so is the label of `Stat`, the figure beside a PageHeader's title.
