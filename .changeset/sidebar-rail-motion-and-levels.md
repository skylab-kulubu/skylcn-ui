The account row is smaller and squarer: a 28px rounded-square avatar on the icon column, the name and a second line in plain case, its menu opening upwards from the foot of the sidebar. Collapsing to the rail and back no longer remounts the sidebar: every icon keeps its place on one column, the text fades out before the width moves and fades in as it lands, and groups fold with the sidebar and unfold with the text in CSS alone, with no measuring from script mid-animation; only the parts that look different on the rail render again. A remembered collapsed sidebar opens collapsed instead of animating shut, and with reduced motion the width and group heights change at once. SidebarItem takes `children`, a level of items under it, such as an open form's pages under the form. Menu labels, the account row's second line and section names drop the spaced capitals. The console menu loses its visible heading and keeps the name for screen readers. The account and console menus step their rows in from the trigger and back out to it, the farthest first, through the new `stagger-items` utility, then shrink into the trigger's corner. Popups (menus, tooltips, popovers) now animate their scale and slide as designed: the transition listed `transform`, which Tailwind 4's scale and translate utilities do not use, so both had jumped. Tooltip takes `onOpenChange`.
